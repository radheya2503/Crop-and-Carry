Find solutions for common crop diseases and pests in Tamil Nadu.

Paddy Blast

Symptoms: Spindle-shaped lesions on leaves, nodes, and panicles.

Solution: Use resistant varieties and apply fungicides like tricyclazole.

Paddy Brown Spot

Symptoms: Small, oval brown spots on leaves and grains.

Solution: Use disease-free seeds and apply mancozeb fungicide.

Corn Smut

Symptoms: Large, gray galls form on corn ears and leaves.

Solution: Remove infected plants and avoid wounding plants during growth.

Wheat Rust

Symptoms: Reddish-brown pustules appear on leaves and stems.

Banana Black Sigatoka

Symptoms: Dark streaks on leaves that turn into necrotic patches.

Potato Late Blight
